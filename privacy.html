Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

StoreFlea is a retail-business management platform that helps shopkeepers manage inventory, billing, customers, and customer communications including WhatsApp marketing and transactional messaging.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how StoreFlea ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our retail-business management platform, mobile and web applications, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

For any questions, contact us at support@storeflea.com.

2. Information we collect

Account & shop information

  • Owner name, phone number, email, password (hashed).
  • Business name, GSTIN, address, business type.
  • Staff member names, phones, and assigned roles.

Operational data

  • Inventory items, prices, stock movements.
  • Invoices, bills, orders, payments, ledgers.
  • Customer ledger entries (name, phone, address, transactions).
  • WhatsApp marketing templates, campaigns, message statuses.

Device & usage

  • IP address, device type, browser/app version, timestamps of access.
  • Crash logs, performance telemetry.

WhatsApp messaging data

  • Phone numbers of customers receiving messages.
  • Message content, delivery & read receipts.
  • Inbound replies, button taps, opt-outs.
  • Last 24 hours of conversation history is retained for support / audit; older messages are automatically deleted.

3. How we use information

  • To provide and improve the Service.
  • To send transactional notifications (OTPs, order confirmations, invoices) via WhatsApp / SMS / email.
  • To enable shopkeepers to send marketing campaigns to their own customers (with shopkeeper consent and customer opt-out controls).
  • To comply with legal obligations, including tax records and accounting requirements under Indian law.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

4. WhatsApp Business specific

We use the Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API to send and receive messages. By using the Service:

  • Shopkeepers warrant that they have obtained prior consent from their customers before sending marketing messages.
  • Every marketing message includes an Unsubscribe/Subscribe link. One-click unsubscribe immediately stops future marketing from that shop.
  • Customer phone numbers are used solely for messaging by the shopkeeper that owns the relationship; we do not sell or share them with other shopkeepers.
  • Inbound messages from customers are stored only for 24 hours and then automatically deleted.
  • Replying "STOP" / "UNSUBSCRIBE" to any marketing message also opts the customer out.

5. How we share information

We share data only as required to operate the Service or comply with law:

  • Meta Platforms — to deliver WhatsApp messages via the Cloud API.
  • Payment processors (e.g., Razorpay) — to process subscription payments.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., AWS) — for hosting and storage.
  • Law enforcement — when legally required, with proper process.

We never sell personal data to third parties.

6. Data retention

  • Account, business, and financial records: retained as long as the account is active, plus periods required by applicable accounting / tax law.
  • WhatsApp message logs (chat viewer): 24 hours.
  • Templates, campaigns, opt-out lists: retained until manually deleted.
  • Encrypted credentials (e.g., shopkeeper WhatsApp tokens): deleted immediately when the user removes the sender.

7. Security

  • Access tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM with keys held separately from the database.
  • All transport is over HTTPS / TLS.
  • Webhook payloads from Meta are verified using HMAC signatures.
  • Access to internal systems is permissioned and audit-logged.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive a portable copy of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact support@storeflea.com.

9. Cookies

The Service uses essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified via email or in-app notice at least 14 days before they take effect.
